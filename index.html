Projects

Phishing Domain Classifier

Python, scikit-learn

This started as a team course project at Pace, and I wrote the implementation. The first version scored 99.46% accuracy, and that number came from a data leak: every benign example was a bare homepage while the phishing examples were full links, so the model learned that anything after the domain meant phishing. I rebuilt it to score the domain name only, with train and test splits grouped by phishing campaign, a time-based test, and recall measured at fixed false positive rates. Averaged over 10 splits, a character n-gram model catches 27.7% of phishing domains at a 1% false positive rate, against 12.7 to 13.7% for models on hand-built features. An audit traced much of that gain to abused TLDs, which change quickly, and to phishing-kit wording reused across campaigns.
