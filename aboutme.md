{% include sec-head.html n="01" id="why" title="Why forensics" %}
<div class="prose" markdown="1">
Digital forensics is the part that holds my attention: collecting and preserving evidence so it still means something later, and the tools that make that possible, like KAPE for artifact collection and Volatility 3 for memory analysis. With my ITS supervisor, I collected forensic artifacts from a potentially compromised lab workstation, then escalated the collection to Pace's security team. That is the work I want to do more of.
</div>
